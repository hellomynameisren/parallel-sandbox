# Parallel: area map sandbox

A sculptable terrain sandbox for a world in progress. Everything runs in your browser: rain, rivers,
lakes and ecology are recomputed after each brush stroke, and the Time section runs plates, erosion
and ice ages forward. Place shield or explosive volcanoes, erupt lava and ash, build islands and watch vegetation
recover. Your terrain, vents and snapshots are saved on your own device only.

Built from the Worldbuilder plugin's Map Sandbox panel.
